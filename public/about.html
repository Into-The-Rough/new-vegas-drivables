Ethos & development

More ways to get around New Vegas, with reasons to go out and find them.

New Vegas Drivables adds cars, motorcycles, aircraft, boats, horses and a few less sensible ways to travel. Finding a ride, getting it working and choosing where to take it are part of the mod.

Make the vehicles belong somewhere

The placements use existing places, props and wrecks wherever practical. A repairable vehicle should make sense where you find it. The buried Highwayman stays a wreck until you restore it. Covered vehicles can wait quietly for someone with the tools and skill to bring them back.

World edits are kept small where possible to reduce conflicts with other mods. That is a design preference, not a promise that every combination of mods will work together.

Give discovery and progression a purpose

Some rides are easy to pick up. Others need Repair skill and parts, a completed quest or a race win. The B-29 follows Volare!, the Hover Bike follows Old World Blues, and the Formula Racer rewards a first race victory.

Horses follow the same idea. An ordinary horse can be tamed without a Survival gate. More demanding horses need higher Survival and have useful differences in speed, endurance and nerve. A rare horse is something to look out for.

Acquisition uses discoveries, repairs and rewards without adding voiced purchase conversations.

Keep the upkeep understandable

Petrol, diesel and biofuel share Jerry Cans. Cell-powered rides accept Energy Cells and Microfusion Cells, with one Microfusion Cell providing twice the charge. The nuclear Corvega uses Reactor Coolant. The Alien Recon Craft needs no fuel.

Repairs use generic supplies such as Car Repair Tools instead of a catalogue of individual mechanical parts. The aim is to make preparing for a trip matter while keeping the requirements easy to remember.

Let different rides feel different

A motorcycle has wheelies, stoppies and burnouts. A skateboard has its own kicks, manuals and jumps. Horses have stamina and a bond to build. Aircraft need room to fly and land. A Hopper is still a Hopper.

Sound is part of that character. The vehicle banks use recordings from real cars, motorcycles, tanks and aircraft, along with designed effects. A recording donor is a choice about sound. It does not rewrite the vehicle’s Fallout power source.

Companions can share suitable vehicles or ride horses with you. The journey should still work with your party.

Build, drive, adjust

Development includes checking placements in game, moving vehicles that sit awkwardly, testing repair and reward interactions, and revisiting handling and sound. A neat position on a map is only a starting point.

The mod has been tested on Steam Deck, with controller support as part of the controls. Development and testing have been carried out with Viva New Vegas in mind.

The F10 menu brings controls, the garage, horse management and racing together. Camera and display settings let you adjust how much movement and information you want on screen.

This site explains the available rides and how to use them. The guides include practical limitations where they affect a trip. Development videos show the mod in motion.

The source will be released under GPLv3. The vehicle creation tools will also be public, so other people can add their own drivables. See the FAQ for release plans and compatibility questions.
