Stunts

Choose a ride for its tricks and controls. These are action names, so your own bindings still apply. Check them in F10 → Controls → Keys.

Records & XP

Jumps, wheelies, flips, flight manoeuvres and arena challenges can set personal bests. Open F10 → Controls → Records to see them for each ride type.

With Stunt XP enabled, a qualifying stunt can award up to 50 XP. Bigger and cleaner attempts pay more. Repeating the same stunt pays less, so try different challenges.

Races

Road racing, buggies, bicycles and horses. Set up a race from anywhere through F10 → Racing → Set up a race.

Get onto the grid

  1. Choose a course and your ride.
  2. Set 1 to 10 laps and 1 to 7 opponents. Use a random field, the same ride as yours, or a custom roster.
  3. Choose debris and raider levels where the course supports them.
  4. Start the race. Starting on foot provides a loan ride. To use your own, select the ride you are already driving.

The starting countdown holds the field in place. Pass checkpoints in order and finish every lap. Afterwards, use the results screen for a rematch or Return Home.

Fuel & stamina

A loan vehicle starts with a full supply. Your own vehicle keeps its fuel level, so refuel before entering. Repairs and refuelling are blocked during the race.

Running dry and coming to a stop causes a DNF. Bicycles use AP to sprint. Horses use their own stamina, which matters when jumping fences.

Race settings

Cars can slipstream behind moving rivals. Bicycles do not receive that benefit. Optional light catch-up helps trailing opponents without slowing the leader.

You can change crowd size, crowd volume and countdown or checkpoint sounds in the racing settings.

Horse guide

Three wild herds with two to four horses each. Bay, black, grey, dapple grey, palomino and white coats can appear at any herd.

Show herds on the map

Find & tame

  1. Inspect a wild horse to see its traits and Survival requirement.
  2. Meet the requirement and complete the calming interaction while out of combat. No items are needed.
  3. Mount the tamed horse. To register it, open F10 → Horses → Add to Stable.

Taming checks effective Survival, including temporary bonuses. You do not need to repeat the skill check on later rides.

Choose a horse

Speed changes its pace. Endurance reduces stamina use and improves recovery. Nerve helps it resist panic and stay controllable when frightened.

Traits are rated out of five. Coat colour does not determine ability. Herd 1 guarantees a horse with no skill gate. Herd 3 guarantees a Survival 75 horse.

Rare horses have a 5% chance on eligible new spawns, in any coat. Look for a name such as “Rare Wild Bay Horse”. A rare needs Survival 100 and has 5/5 in every trait. Only one untamed rare can exist at a time.

Riding & stamina

Tap Gait up / Sprint to move into a faster gait. Tap Brake to slow down a gait. Hold Sprint for a burst of speed, Jump to clear an obstacle, and Stop to halt.

Give the horse time to recover between sprints. Endurance and your bond improve recovery. Hold the mapped view control to look around without steering with the camera.

Stable & whistle

The first living horse you register becomes active. Adding another horse does not change that selection. Choose Make Active in F10 → Horses to switch.

The whistle calls your active horse. Riding an unregistered horse does not replace it. You can name horses and remove them from the stable through the same menu.

Feeding, care & trust

Use the horse command wheel to feed, pet or brush a horse. Horse Feed restores health and stamina and is consumed when used. A dirty horse offers Brush in place of Pet.

Riding and meaningful care build your bond through Unfamiliar, Familiar, Trusted and Close. Greater trust helps recovery and reduces panic. Repeated care has a reward cooldown.

While riding, use Reassure / Pat to calm your horse.

Following, waiting & saddlebags

The command wheel lets you ask a horse to Follow or Wait, open its saddlebags, and manage its active status. Use a hitching post to tether it when leaving it behind.

Calm horses can look around, graze on suitable ground and settle while waiting. Their personality and coat condition appear in the stable.

Death & herd replacement

Ordinary wild horses replenish after three continuous in-game days away. Tamed and rare horses stay dead. A later new horse can roll rare again.

Wildlife normally ignores horses, but they can still take damage. Treat your own horse’s death as permanent.

Horse racing

Choose Mojave Steeplechase in F10 → Racing → Set up a race. The course has eight fences. Balance your pace against stamina so you can keep jumping through the lap.

Fuel supplies

Jerry Cans
Used by liquid-fuel vehicles, including the Vertibird. Petrol, diesel and biofuel share the same supply. Partly used cans are consumed. Bulk refuelling is supported.
Energy Cells / Microfusion Cells
Charge the Highwayman, Hover Bike and Camp Golf Cart. One Microfusion Cell supplies twice the charge of one Energy Cell.
Reactor Coolant
Used by the nuclear Corvega.
No supply
The Alien Recon Craft needs no fuel or coolant. Human-powered rides use no fuel.

Restoring a vehicle

Inspect a covered vehicle or original wreck to see its repair requirements. Some discoveries only need Repair skill. Others also need one or two Car Repair Tools. The crashed Vertibird needs a Sensor Module.

Bring restoration parts in your inventory. Required components are consumed when restoration succeeds. Each map entry lists the exact requirements.

Travel notes

The OWB reward Hover Bike is placed in Big MT. Bringing that bike back through the Transportalponder is not implemented.

The B-29 unlocks after Volare!, but its reported runway / ground-support problem remains open. The Hopper’s corrected Strip-door transfer still needs an in-game round-trip check.
