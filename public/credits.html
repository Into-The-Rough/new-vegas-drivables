Credits & sources

The mod

New Vegas Drivables is a Fallout: New Vegas mod by intotherough. Watch the mod on Oliver Swanick Productions.

The guide

Locations, restoration requirements and unlock routes come from the mod’s placement records and implementation notes. Vehicle speeds use the same settings and conversion as the F10 garage. Circuit diagrams follow the race routes. Vehicle renders and showcase artwork come from the mod’s own assets and videos. The amber text, selection outlines and tabs follow the in-game F10 menu.

Map & game

The Mojave map artwork and Fallout: New Vegas belong to their respective rights holders. This is an unofficial fan project.

Font

Monofonto by Ray Larabie, the typeface used for the game’s menu text. This site includes the unmodified 2.000 release and its original readme and licence, which permits use and redistribution. The original package is available from Abstract Fonts. The designer’s current catalogue is at Typodermic Fonts.
